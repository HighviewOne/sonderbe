# Agent pitch — DRAFT (validation stage)

Goal of this email: get a 15-minute call and learn whether agents would pay, and how much.
It is not a hard sell. Send to short-sale / distressed-property agents in LA, Orange and
Riverside first. Personalize the first line every time.

---

**Subject:** Homeowners facing foreclosure in [County] who asked to talk to an agent

Hi [First name],

I saw [your short-sale listing on Elm St / your reviews mentioning short sales / your
team's distressed-property page], so I thought you'd be the right person to ask.

I'm building SonderBe, a free site for Southern California homeowners who just received a
Notice of Default. It walks them through their options and a checklist of the documents
a lender will ask for: pay stubs, tax returns, hardship letter and so on. When they're
ready, they can ask to be connected with **one** local real estate professional.

For agents, that means:

- **Opt-in leads only.** Every homeowner has asked to be contacted, in writing.
- **Exclusive.** Once you accept a lead, no other agent sees it.
- **Pre-qualified on paperwork.** Before you call, you can see how far along they are
  (e.g. "income docs 8/11 ready, hardship letter not started").
- **Your county only.** You choose the counties you serve.

Homeowners never pay SonderBe, and agents on the platform agree not to charge homeowners
any upfront fee.

I'm signing up a small group of founding agents before launch, and I'd like your honest
opinion first: would this be worth a monthly subscription to you, and what would make it
a clear yes or no? Do you have 15 minutes this week or next?

Thanks,
[Your name]
SonderBe · [phone] · [sonderbe.vercel.app or your domain]

## Questions to ask on the call

1. How do you find short-sale / pre-foreclosure clients today, and what does it cost you per closed deal?
2. What makes a lead worth calling, and what makes it a waste of time?
3. Does seeing a homeowner's document readiness change how you'd prioritize them?
4. Monthly subscription vs. pay per accepted lead: which would you choose?
5. At what price would this be a no-brainer? At what price would you say no?
6. Would you want leads in more than one county? How many active leads could you handle?
7. Anything that would make you uncomfortable about how homeowners are contacted?

Write down the numbers. If 2–3 of the first 10 agents say they'd pay, keep going.

## Notes before sending

- Fill in the price only after the first few calls. Ask open-ended first.
- Don't say "licensed agents" or "vetted" until DRE license checks are built.
- Don't promise lead volume until mail outreach is running and you know the response rate.
- A custom domain (e.g. sonderbe.com) will look more credible than `vercel.app`.
